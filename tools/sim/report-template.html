Economy, progression and balance audit · 7 November 2026

Hordefall Balance Audit

A year of play simulated with the game's own code: five kinds of player, five upgrade strategies, 30 economy experiments and a Monte Carlo of crest luck. Nothing in the live game, the database or any save was touched.

Verdict

Complete, generous early, and then a long slow race between two growth rates

Every system the design calls for is live, the first month is fast and rewarding, and no free player runs short of Gems. The weak part is the long game. After about day 90, stage progress is decided almost entirely by Ember cost growth against enemy growth, so playing six times as much, spending Gems well or paying $300 a month ends the year within a few hundred stages of a casual player. Stars, rarity and most rewards barely move the stage, players sit on one wall for weeks, Trial levels 30 to 40 are out of reach, and several currencies pile up with nothing to buy.

How this was done

A separate simulator that runs the real game code

The simulator in tools/sim/ loads the game's own GameState, formulas and reward tables and plays them on a fast clock with fixed random seeds. It has no network, no Supabase and no saves. Economy experiments edit a copy of the source code, never the game itself. Every number on this page comes from those runs or straight from the code.

Player profiles

    What is real and what is modelled

    • Real code: every cost, reward, drop table, odds, hero stat, Ember and Rekindle formula, Trials, relics, weapons, Scouting, Glyphs, Rush, Colosseum rewards, Guild Shop, Observatory, Market Row, offline earnings.
    • Modelled: battles are maths (damage per second against the wave and boss model, same rule as the game's boss check), not Phaser. The guild is assumed to be 20 players about as strong as you. Colosseum foes come from the game's own offline opponent roll.
    • Hypothetical: there is no real-money shop, so paying players get 100 Gems per US dollar, spread over the year.
    • Not simulated: Expedition (hidden in the game), Ultimate casting, Hunting Journal kill bonuses.

    Profiles A to D ran 3 seeds for 365 days; paying profiles and strategies ran 1 seed. Medians are shown; ranges are the lowest and highest seed. Crest luck used 5,000 simulated players.

    Phase 8 · Ranked problems

    Top 10 balance problems

    critical1. Late-game progress is a race between two growth rates

    What is wrong
    From about day 90, every profile gains roughly 80 to 100 stages a month and the year ends with all of them between stage 4,900 and 5,350.
    Why
    Rekindle pays 6% more Embers per stage, but one more stage needs about two more Ember levels, which cost 14.5% more. Each run pays relatively less than the last, so progress flattens into a slow log curve that more play time barely bends.
    Formula or value
    EMBER_COST_GROWTH = 1.07, ENEMY_GROWTH = 1.07, payout 40·1.06^(stage−126), power per level 2^(L/20) (×1.035 a level, about half a stage). At day 90: Ember growth 1.065 gives stage 7,950 and 1.075 gives 3,000 (current 4,350); enemy growth 1.068 gives 6,900 and 1.072 gives 3,150.
    Player impact
    Casual A plays 25 minutes a day and ends on 4,900; optimized C plays 2.5 hours with 5 times the Rekindles and ends on 5,300. Effort matters a lot in month one and very little after month three.
    Recommended
    Decide how fast the late game should be, then move only these two values. A mild option is EMBER_COST_GROWTH 1.07 → 1.068 (re-simulated below). A structural option is to let stars scale Ember levels (problem 2).
    Expected effect
    See the revised year: Ember growth 1.068 lifts the whole late curve; the star link widens the gap between invested and casual players.

    high2. Stars, relics, weapons, Gems and money hardly move the stage

    What is wrong
    Every upgrade except Ember levels is a flat multiplier, and enemies grow 7% a stage, so even big multipliers are worth only a few stages.
    Why
    A star is ×1.5, which is ln 1.5 / ln 1.07 ≈ 6 stages. A full 1★ → 7★ hero is ×11 ≈ 36 stages. Late in the year the player is at stage 5,000.
    Formula or value
    Stars ×1.5 (STAR_STAT_MULT), pink stars ×1.5, weapons ×1.15 a level. Experiments: star costs −30% → −50 stages; +20% → −150; chest odds, Gem income ±20%, pack prices −20% → within ±50.
    Player impact
    The $300-a-month player has a full 7★ team on day 187, which no free player reaches in the year, and still ends on stage 5,000, below free B (5,350). A big star-up does not get a player past the wall they are stuck on.
    Recommended
    Proposed: each star above 1★ makes that hero's Ember levels count 2% more in battle (heroLevel, 7★ = +12%). Keep stars mattering where levels don't (Colosseum, Guild Boss, Rush), as today.
    Expected effect
    Stars turn into hundreds of stages late in the year instead of dozens, so collecting and paying feel worth it. See the starEmber run below.

    high3. Element bosses are hard walls with no way around them

    What is wrong
    From stage 600, every 100th boss takes damage only from its weakness element. A team without that element can never pass.
    Why
    This is the owner's rule from PR #95. It is fine for a mixed team, but nothing else in the game punishes a team choice this hard.
    Formula or value
    bossImmuneTo() in data.ts. A one-element team stays on stage 700 for 180 days; a rarest-heroes team stays on 2,000. With 20% damage from other elements (experiment) they reach 4,400 and 4,500.
    Player impact
    A player who builds a favourite team hits a permanent stop and may not know why; Gems pile up (24,000 by day 180) because nothing they buy helps.
    Recommended
    Keep the rule, and make the reason impossible to miss: the boss button and the loss screen say "Only Dark heroes can hurt Aureth" with the player's best Dark hero. If the owner agrees, also let other elements do 20% damage (elementBossSoft).
    Expected effect
    No change for normal teams (+100 stages for B at day 180); stuck teams go from never passing to normal progress.

    high4. Rarity barely changes power

    What is wrong
    An Epic has about 10% more base power than a Common, but costs 8 times the crests to unlock and drops far less often.
    Why
    Base stats come from the hero Type, not rarity. Stars are the only big multiplier, and Commons get stars fastest.
    Formula or value
    Median power at level 100: Epic ≈ Common +10%. Unlock 10 / 30 / 80 crests; chest odds 70 / 22 / 8.
    Player impact
    Every simulated team at day 365 is mostly 7★ Commons. The $300-a-month player owns four 7★ Ascendant heroes and one 6★ and leaves all of them on the bench. The rarest-heroes strategy is the worst one.
    Recommended
    Proposed: base ATK and HP ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2.25 / ×3.4 for Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary (heroAtk, heroHp in formulas.ts), about one star per rarity step.
    Expected effect
    Same stage pace (−150 at day 90, inside the noise) but rarer heroes, Legendaries and Ascendant forms become upgrades worth chasing.

    medium5. Trial levels 30 to 40 are out of reach

    What is wrong
    Every profile stops at Trial level 27 to 29 for the rest of the year.
    Why
    Levels need 5 heroes at an Ember level. The needs jump from 4,900 (level 30) to 6,300 (31) and on to 14,000 (40), while players reach about 4,600 in a year.
    Formula or value
    TRIAL_REQS in data.ts: [5, 3700], [5, 4100], [5, 4500], [5, 4900], [5, 6300], [5, 6900], [5, 7550] …
    Player impact
    The Trials list shows ten levels nobody will see; after about day 120 Trials stop offering anything new.
    Recommended
    Proposed: levels 27 to 33 need 3,550 / 3,800 / 4,050 / 4,300 / 4,600 / 4,900 / 5,300 (trialReqSmooth). Look at 34 to 40 again once the late pace is decided.
    Expected effect
    A new Trial level every month or two through year one.

    medium6. Several currencies pile up with nothing to buy

    What is wrong
    By day 365 the active player holds about 72,000 Enhance Stones, 366,000 Ore and Crystal, 135,000 Warsteel and 10^12 Essence.
    Why
    Their sinks run out: team weapons are all level 9 by about day 170, relic enhancements finish, guild relics cap.
    Formula or value
    Trial Stones 1 a win from level 6, 2 from 26, 3 from 36. Weapons max at level 9.
    Player impact
    Rewards that buy nothing feel worthless, and the reward screens fill with numbers that don't matter.
    Recommended
    Proposed: Trial Stones 1 a win from level 16 and 2 from 36 (trialStonesHalf). Later, add a long sink for Ore, Crystal and Warsteel (not simulated, a design task).
    Expected effect
    Stones on hand at day 90 drop from 12,500 to 7,300 with no change in progress.

    medium7. A particular Rare or Epic hero is a lottery

    What is wrong
    From random drops alone, an active player unlocks a chosen Epic around day 213 and only 44% get it to 3★ in a year.
    Why
    Chests and Trials spread crests over 107 heroes; only targeted crests (Wild Crests, shop offers, packs, vouchers) can be aimed.
    Formula or value
    Hero Chest: 1.8 Gems per Common crest but 1,016 Gems per crest of one chosen Epic.
    Player impact
    Players who open chests for a favourite Epic are let down; the good buys are not the obvious ones.
    Recommended
    Not a value change: label chests as "random hero", point players to Wild Crests and the Hero Shop, and consider a pity counter later.
    Expected effect
    Fewer wasted Gems for players like D.

    medium8. Big spenders run out of things to buy

    What is wrong
    At $300 a month (hypothetical) the player has 39,890 Gems unspent at day 365 after maxing every weekly Ascendant Chest.
    Why
    Gem sinks are capped per week or per slot. There is no real-money shop yet.
    Formula or value
    Ascendant Chest 10 buys a week (5,350 Gems); monthly pack once a month.
    Player impact
    Only matters once purchases exist.
    Recommended
    Plan the real-money shop around time-limited packs and cosmetics rather than more of the same sinks.
    Expected effect
    Design input for later, no change now.

    low9. Gem income depends mostly on tapping chests

    What is wrong
    Gold chests are 58% of an active player's Gems. A casual player earns about 23,000 a year against 83,000 for an optimized one.
    Why
    Gold chest Gems (5 each, 150 a day) only come while playing with the screen on.
    Formula or value
    GOLD_CHEST_GEMS_PER_DAY = 150; floating chests every 25 to 45 s, 10% gold.
    Player impact
    Casual players miss the biggest Gem source without knowing it.
    Recommended
    Keep the cap; show "Gems from chests today: 85 / 150" on the Home panel so players know it is there.
    Expected effect
    More casual players collect it.

    low10. Gold-side upgrades and boosts do almost nothing

    What is wrong
    Gold ±20%, hero level cost ±30% and boss HP −20% all change day-90 stage by 50 to 150, inside the run-to-run noise.
    Why
    Gold levels reset every Rekindle; Embers carry the run.
    Formula or value
    Hourglass 40 Gems, Gold Boost 50 Gems, Rekindle boost 80/160 Gems.
    Player impact
    Players who buy boosts (profile D spent 3,050 Gems on them) get nothing measurable.
    Recommended
    Move boosts lower in the shop or make the Rekindle boost pay Embers ×2 for Gems only when it is worth it. Market Row stays as the owner decided.
    Expected effect
    Fewer wasted Gems.

    Roadmap

    What to do, in order

    Critical

    • Decide the late-game pace: keep it, or try Ember growth 1.068 and/or the star link (options 4 and 5).
    • Make element-boss walls explain themselves (UI hint).

    High

    • Rarity base stats (change 2).
    • Smooth Trial levels 27–33 (change 1).

    Medium

    • Trim Trial Stones (change 3).
    • Design a sink for Ore, Crystal and Warsteel.
    • Show chest Gem progress on Home; move boosts down the shop.

    Long-term

    • Real-money shop built around new sinks, not more of the capped ones.
    • Look at Trial levels 34–40 and Ascendant final forms once the late pace is set.
    • Re-run this simulator after each balance change (tools/sim).
