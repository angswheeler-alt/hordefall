# Hordefall

Web-first idle RPG prototype (see the game design doc). This build covers the core loop only:

- 23 heroes auto-fight down a lane toward the enemy crystal, five at a time. Fire: Flare, Ember, Flint, Cinder. Earth: Briar, Fern, Thicket, Moss. Water: Brine, Brook, Ripple, Marina. Dark: Shade, Vex, Rook, Dread. Light: Dawn, Lumen, Sol, Aegis. Those 20 are Common, four per element; three Rare heroes have kits of their own: Wraith (Dark Ranger), Nyx (Dark Support, the Dark healer) and Seraph (Light Magician). Every hero has one of ten Types (combat archetypes, like the original's): Ranger, Gunner, Magician, Knight, Barbarian, Rogue, Lancer, Samurai, Brawler and Support. The Type sets the stat shape (`TYPE_PROFILES` in `profiles.ts`), only Knights get the Knight shield, and every Support in the lineup rallies the team (+15% ATK, `SUPPORT_RALLY`). Star skills are data (each SP1/SP2/Aura has an effect in `data.ts`, totalled in `skills.ts`). Heroes outside the first five take their kits from Crush Them All heroes under our own names (Flare, Flint, Cinder, Briar and Moss so far): Signatures such as Sticky Spores (slow) and Hawkeye, procs such as Big Boom, the Heat Strike and Forest Strength Ultimates, and Auras that reach only one element (`Effect.element`). Saves from the old five-hero roster carry over: Marin, Bramble, Pyra, Nox and Solenne become Brine, Thicket, Ember, Shade and Lumen. Every hero is frame-animated from generated sprite sheets (run and attack).
- Stats double every 20 levels. Type chart from the original game: Water > Fire > Earth > Water deal 4x; Light and Dark deal 2x to each other. Each element deals 0.5x to one element (the reverse of its matchup, or its own element for Light/Dark) and 1x to the rest.
- A boss every 50 stages with a 30 s timer. Losing drops you back a stage to farm until you tap "Fight boss".
- Ultimates (40x damage) unlock at level 30 as a stand-in for 3 stars. 2x speed and auto-ult toggles.
- Offline earnings for up to 12 hours: gold at your recent rate, plus stages up to the next boss.
- Rekindle (prestige) unlocks after clearing stage 125: reset to stage 1 and gold level 1 for Embers (40 at stage 126, +6% per stage beyond). Embers buy Ember levels (each 7% dearer than the last, so a Rekindle's gain shrinks and progress levels off near stage 2,000 until stars and relics push it) that add to a hero's gold level and stay through Rekindle; they stay on the hero for good. The Rekindle button opens a rewards page: Embers with the stage and player-level bonuses, a locked Market Row section, the 16-slot Rekindle Wheel (a slot every 50 stages of this run from 150; each slot spins once at Rekindle for a Hero Chest, 3 Trial tickets, 40 Trial Essence or +5% Embers, never Gems), the player level bar (lifetime Embers; +10% Embers per level and a free Hero Chest each level) and Rekindle / 2x for 80 Gems / 3x for 160 Gems.
- Balance: the stage 100 boss needs a lead hero around level 100 (gold + Ember). Bosses are weak to Water until stage 600; from there each 100th-stage boss is weak to a rotating element: Water 600, Earth 700, Fire 800, Light 900, Dark 1000, then repeating.
- Hero Chest (30 Gems) gives Hero Crests (medals) for 3 random heroes. Heroes unlock at 1★ and Crests evolve them up to 8★ (1★→2★ 10, then 50, 250, 600, 1,500, 2,500 and 5,000 for 7★→8★): each star multiplies stats by 1.5 (the original doubles; Samuel chose x1.5 so late stars add mechanics); 2★/3★ unlock two skills, 4★ a team aura, 5★ the Ultimate+, 6★ the Signature+ (standard heroes), 7★ the hero's Type Mastery (`TYPE_MASTERY`), 8★ Ascended (Ultimate recharges in 20 s instead of 30). At 8★ every 10 extra medals for that hero become a Legendary medal (for Legendary heroes later). Free Gems follow the original's sources (`src/rewards.ts`): a 60-Gem start, gold chests (5), daily challenges (5 a day at 5 Gems, up to 25), a 5-day login calendar (50 Gems on day 5; gold or a Hero Chest on other days), a daily Lucky Spin, tiered achievements and one-time Bootcamp goals. A free Daily Medal Bundle (3 heroes, 5/3/2 medals by rarity, Hero Chest odds) opens each day once 3 daily challenges are done. Guilds (`src/guild.ts`, server rules in `supabase/guilds.sql`) open to signed-in players after their first Rekindle: up to 30 members with a leader and officers, chat, and medal requests. Each player can ask for one hero's medals every 8 hours (Common 20, Rare 10, Epic 5; no Legendary). Guildmates give up to 5 each and earn Guild Coins per medal (3/6/12 by rarity), spent in the guild shop: 4 daily hero offers (Common 10 medals for 50, Rare 6 for 80, Epic 3 for 120, each once a day), Wild Medals for any non-Legendary hero the player picks (8/15/30 each, 20 a week), and always in stock a Hero Chest (60), an Enhance Stone (40), 5 Trial tickets (30), an Ember pouch (80, 1% of lifetime Embers, 3 a day), a Gold Banner (+50% enemy gold) and a War Horn (+25% hero damage) (50 each, 30 minutes, stack to 2 hours). The Guild Boss is one shared boss per guild per day (UTC, element rotates): every member gets 3 attempts with their Trial team (Ember levels), each paying 10 + level Guild Coins (doubled for a full 1/15 share of its health) and 30 minutes of gold; a beaten boss pays everyone who hit it 5 x (10 + level) Guild Coins, a Hero Chest and 1% of lifetime Embers, and comes back a level higher. Guild Research gives every member shared bonuses up to rank 10 (rank r costs 300 x r² points, 115,500 for all ten): Combat +2% hero damage, Prosperity +5% gold from enemies, Exploration +5% Trial Essence and Enhance Stones, Elemental Studies +3% damage in Trials and against the Guild Boss, each per rank. Members put Guild Coins in (1 coin = 1 point) and every boss attempt adds 10 + level points to the focus branch the leader or officers pick. Expedition research comes with Expeditions. Guild Quests reset every Monday (UTC): defeat enemies, clear stages, win Trials and Guild Boss attempts, with goals of 2,000 / 150 / 10 / 12 per member (at least 5 members counted). The game reports its counts every minute. A shared track pays 40/60/80/120 Guild Coins (plus a Hero Chest at 100%) at each 25% of average completion to members who helped, and doing half a member's share of a quest pays 15 Guild Coins.
- Big numbers use break_infinity.js. Saves to localStorage, and signed-in players also sync to the cloud (see Online accounts).

- Hero stat pages (like the original's three pages, on the hero page): Atk/HP/Def with base and bonus, crit rate and damage, attacks per second, range, move speed, Effect Resistance (base 20%, the chance to shrug off a boss's 15% stun), Frenzy (base 5%: attack twice as fast for 3 s), dodge, stun, AoE radius and damage (splash heroes), Ultimate Atk, Knight Shield HP (Knights start each stage and life with a 20%-of-HP shield), Bonus Gold, and freeze (enemy stops, then bursts for 1x ATK nearby), burn (0.2x ATK/s for 3 s) and poison (0.1x ATK/s per stack, up to 5, for 4 s) chances, times and damage. GameState.heroStats is the one calculation battle and the page both use (src/stats.ts holds the base numbers). Relic sets add stun/freeze/burn/poison time and Knight shield like the original.
- Hero stat profiles (src/profiles.ts): each role has its own crit, crit damage, dodge, Frenzy, Effect Resistance and Defense shape, and each hero adds one specialty with a trade-off (e.g. Shade: 25% dodge and crit but low HP; Moss: poisons, low HP; Aegis: 6x Defense, less ATK). Builds: freeze (Marina, Ripple, Brook), burn (Ember, Flare, Sol), poison (Moss, Briar, Shade), stun (Cinder, Vex, Dawn). Two heroes sharing an effect in the lineup make it last 25% longer; three also add +50% effect damage (stun: lasts longer still).
- Defense: every hero has ATK, HP and Defense (Defense grows with level and stars; by default 0.1% of base HP, so tanks defend best). A hit lands for hit² / (hit + Defense): Defense equal to the hit halves it, and nothing blocks a hit fully. Relic sets add a lot of it.
- Relics tab (the original's artifacts, following the Crush Them All wiki's list with our own names): 7 normal relics (1★-4★), six 4★ sets and five 5★ sets of three pieces, 40 relics in all. Pieces have one or two effects (team or element ATK/HP/Defense, attack speed, crit, dodge, gold, Stall income/cost/speed, hero level cost, Embers); the wiki's numbers are reached at level 20. status-duration bonuses wait for those systems; six pieces the wiki leaves blank are guessed from their set. Crafted with Trial Essence (set pieces also take 2-3 Enhance Stones). Levelling any N★ relic to 20 opens (N+1)★ relics. At the cap, Enhance Stones raise it by 5, up to 4 times. Set bonuses when every piece is crafted. Two slots free, up to 22 with Gems and stage gates. Hero level discounts work without a slot. Relics stay through Rekindle.

## Run

    npm install
    npm run dev      # http://localhost:5173
    npm run build    # static build in dist/
    npm test         # unit tests (saves, economy, medals, relics, Rekindle); also run on every pull request

Stack: Phaser 3 + TypeScript + Vite. Lane in Phaser, menus in HTML/CSS. Balance knobs live in `src/formulas.ts`.
- Bottom tab bar like the original: Heroes (wood-styled hero rows with Level Up buttons, Manage Team, Hero Chest and Collection), Market Row, Relics, Battle (Trials and run stats), Shop (login calendar, Lucky Spin, daily challenges, Daily Medal Bundle, Hero Chest, Bootcamp, achievements) and Guild.
- Market Row tab: ten Stalls (Bakery to Star Bazaar) opened and upgraded with gold. Each fills a bar on a timer; tap to collect until level 50, then it collects itself (also while offline). Income x3 every 50 Stall levels. Stalls reset on Rekindle; each Rekindle adds +25% Stall income and +5 levels (up to 600) to auto-collect Stalls, shown on the Rekindle page. Buy x1 / x10 / max and a Level all button that spreads gold across open Stalls, cheapest level first, like on the Heroes tab.
- Manage Team (Heroes tab): choose which recruited heroes fight (up to 5). Taking a hero out drops them to gold level 1 and refunds that gold; their Ember levels stay (Embers also count in Trials and PvP).
- Elemental Trials (Battle tab, open after the stage 50 boss): one dungeon per element on a weekly schedule (Dark Mon/Fri/Sun, Light Tue/Thu/Sat, Earth Tue/Thu/Sun, Water Wed/Fri/Sun, Fire Mon/Wed/Sat), 26 levels unlocked one by one (Samuel's table: Essence 20 doubling each level, difficulty 1 to 3,600, from level 10 a requirement of N heroes at an Ember level, e.g. 2x100 up to 5x4000), up to 20 tickets at one per 30 minutes (a loss keeps the ticket). Trials use Ember levels and stars only, not gold levels. Wins drop that element's Essence (for Relics) and as many medals (Hero Crests) as the level number, spread over random heroes of that element: level 20 pays 20, so high Trials are the main way to raise stars past the Rekindle wall.

## Online accounts

Accounts and cloud saves use [Supabase](https://supabase.com). Without keys the game runs as before, with local saves only.

1. Create a free Supabase project.
2. In the SQL editor, run `supabase/setup.sql` once. It creates the `saves` table with row-level security, so each player can only read and write their own save. Then run `supabase/guilds.sql` once for guilds (safe to run again).
3. Under Authentication > Sign In / Providers > Email, switch off **Confirm email**. Players use a username, which the game turns into a placeholder login address that never receives mail.
4. Put the project URL and the anon public key in `src/config.ts`. Both are meant to be public; the security rules do the protecting.

Players sign in from the Account section in Settings (gear at the top right of the map) with a username and password (no email). There is no password reset yet, since there's no email to send it to. Guests keep playing locally. On the first sign-in on a device that already has progress, the player picks which save to keep. After that, the save uploads every 30 seconds and when the tab is hidden, and when the game starts the most recently played device's save wins.

Saves are trusted from the client for now, so a determined player could edit their own save. Server-side checks come with PvP and leaderboards.

## Medal drop rates

Hero Chest (30 Gems, 3 drops): each drop is Common 70%, Rare 22% or Epic 8%, then a random hero of that rarity. Medals per drop are Common 5/10/20, Rare 3/6/12, Epic 2/4/8 (60/30/10% chances). Trials give each medal to a hero of the Trial's element: Common 80%, Rare 17%, Epic 3%. Legendary heroes never drop. Rarities with no heroes yet are skipped. All numbers live in `src/data.ts`.

## Unlocking heroes

Brine is free. Every other hero unlocks with Hero Crests (medals) from the Hero Chest and Trials, by rarity: Common 10, Rare 30, Epic 80. Legendary heroes never unlock from Crests and never drop from the Hero Chest; they only come through special means. Unlocking spends the Crests and adds the hero at level 1; gold then levels them as before.

## Art

Backgrounds and hero and boss animations are built by scripts in `art/` (no dependencies):

- `node art/backgrounds/build.js` writes the 15 region layers to `public/bg/`.
- `node art/heroes/animate.js` turns each `art/heroes/<id>/` folder with `run_sheet.png` and `attack_sheet.png` (one row of frames on flat magenta, e.g. from an image generator) into `public/heroes/<id>.png`, `<id>_run.png` and `<id>_attack.png`, and regenerates `src/heroAnims.ts`, which the battle scene uses to play them. Each hero is drawn at their own size (`HERO_SIZE` in the script: brutes and armoured knights big, faeries, bats and bunnies small). To animate another hero, add their folder and run it. A hero whose sheets disagree on a detail can get an `art/heroes/<id>/fix.js` touch-up (see `art/heroes/fixes.js`).

- `node art/bosses/animate.js` does the same for the five bosses (Ignivar, Nerath, Tharos, Nyxara and Aureth). They take turns on every boss stage (`stageBoss`); before 600 they fight without an element (no hero hits them extra hard or soft, and their blows land the same on everyone), and from 600 each fights as the element it looks like, with every 100th stage the element boss: each `art/bosses/<element>/` folder holds `idle_sheet.png`, `attack_sheet.png`, `hurt_sheet.png` (3 frames) and `death_sheet.png`, which become `public/bosses/<element>_<anim>.png` and `src/bossAnims.ts`. In battle the boss idles, plays its attack when it strikes, flinches at most every 4 seconds when hit, and plays its death before fading.

All of them also write review images to a git-ignored `preview/` folder.
